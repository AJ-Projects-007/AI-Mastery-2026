WHO WE ARE

Three generations of stone people

AJ Gemstones started as a single loupe and a notebook of supplier contacts across Sri Lanka, Colombia, and Myanmar. Today we still buy the same way — in person, stone by stone — because a gem's story matters as much as its cut.

We're not a mall counter. Every piece that reaches our shelf has passed through the same three hands: the buyer, the appraiser, and the founder. If we wouldn't gift it to family, we don't sell it.
